# 📝 Assignment 3: Geometric Transformations on Images

## 📋 Problem Statement

Solve the following using Python:

1. **Translation**  
   - Given a point P(x, y) = (2, 3), apply a translation of 4 units to the right and 5 units upwards.  
   - **Question:** What will be the new coordinates of the point after translation?

2. **Rotation**  
   - Given a point P(2, 3), rotate it by 90 degrees counterclockwise around the origin.  
   - **Question:** What are the new coordinates of the point after rotation?

3. **Scaling**  
   - Given a point P(3, 4), apply a scaling factor of 2 along the x-axis and 3 along the y-axis.  
   - **Question:** What will be the new coordinates of the point after scaling?

4. **Shearing**  
   - Given a point P(2, 3), apply a shear along the x-axis with a shear factor of 2.  
   - **Question:** What are the new coordinates of the point after shearing?

5. **Image Transformation (Real-time Data)**  
   - Apply transformation operations: **Translation, Rotation, Scaling, and Shearing** on a real-time image.

---

## 🧠 Concepts Involved

- **Translation:** Moving every point of an object a certain distance in a specified direction.
- **Rotation:** Rotating points around the origin by a specified angle.
- **Scaling:** Changing the size of an object by multiplying coordinates by scale factors.
- **Shearing:** Shifting one coordinate proportional to another, distorting the shape.

---

## 💡 Formulae

- **Translation:**  
  \( x' = x + t_x \)  
  \( y' = y + t_y \)

- **Rotation (90° CCW):**  
  \( x' = -y \)  
  \( y' = x \)

- **Scaling:**  
  \( x' = s_x \cdot x \)  
  \( y' = s_y \cdot y \)

- **Shearing (x-axis):**  
  \( x' = x + sh_x \cdot y \)  
  \( y' = y \)

## 🛠️ Implementation Steps

1. **Calculate new coordinates for each transformation using the above formulae.**
2. **For images:**  
   - Use OpenCV (`cv2`) to apply these transformations on a real image.
